# Living Mechanical Cell

Use this style for visuals that should feel like living infrastructure:
biological organization expressed through precise industrial engineering.
It is built for Little Organs, but reusable for any context where the visual
argument is membrane, selective transport, specialized machinery, and closure.

## Core Effect

Make the viewer feel that cognition has become a physical organism.

The preferred visual argument is:

```text
bounded membrane -> engineered internal functions -> controlled context flow
```

The image should feel premium, legible, and product grade. It should not feel
like a dashboard, a medical illustration, a reactor, or generic sci fi.

## Visual Grammar

- Wide cinematic hero composition, usually 16:9.
- Large engineered cell or membrane object on the right.
- Clean dark copy safe negative space on the left.
- Translucent outer membrane with a crisp cyan or white rim.
- Brushed titanium, dark graphite, glassy black, polished bevels.
- Machined organelles, docking ports, gasket rings, retaining walls, radial
  bridges, sensor grooves, micro conduits, layered service decks.
- Controlled internal complexity. Dense detail must remain organized.
- Subtle cool white or pale cyan glints. Avoid strong neon.
- No green line, no green orb, no central reactor glow unless explicitly asked.

## Composition Pattern

Left side:

- Smooth graphite to black gradient.
- Reserved for future logo, headline, or copy.
- No visible UI, labels, panels, or clutter.

Right side:

- Main object: a mechanical living cell inside a transparent membrane.
- Strong silhouette first, internal detail second.
- The membrane may hold rings, connectors, ports, and nested mechanisms.
- The object should sit on or hover above a polished black surface.
- Reflection is subtle: visible near contact, low contrast, quick fade.

Center of object:

- Treat the core as an intentional architectural cavity, not a black void.
- Add a recessed ground floor platform when useful: dark titanium, graphite,
  concentric rings, radial seams, tiny ports, and restrained edge highlights.
- Preserve depth and shadow. Do not fill the center with a glowing ball.

## Two Level Mechanical Relief

When using the Little Organs logo geometry, preserve this rule:

```text
level 1 = lower outer circular base outside the ridge
level 2 = raised inner circular panels inside the ridge
```

The ridge is the step boundary. The inner circle sits clearly higher than the
outer circle. The height change must be readable through vertical sidewalls,
contact shadows, bevels, and parallax.

Required details:

- Bottom shelf remains extruded and solid.
- Outer rim can be taller as a structural retaining wall.
- Connectors must look engineered, not added afterward.
- Radial bridges should seat into sockets with collars, gasket rings, clamp
  details, keyed tabs, and contact shadows.
- Keep the logo silhouette readable.
- Do not turn the whole logo into towers or random multi tier architecture.

## Background Mood

- Deep black to graphite cinematic studio environment.
- Faint cool blue or cyan atmospheric gradient behind the cell.
- Subtle dimensional depth, never noisy.
- No smoke blanket, particle clutter, cyberpunk city, medical lab backdrop, or
  busy abstract texture.

## Detail Discipline

Use high detail only where it clarifies engineering:

- sidewalls
- rim connections
- docking assemblies
- raised ledges
- recessed service floors
- membrane edge and internal transport rings

Avoid detail that creates noise:

- all over random greebles
- disconnected pipes
- floating cylinders
- unresolved seams
- strong lens flares
- heavy bloom
- blurry foreground objects

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a [format] in the living-mechanical-cell style.

Subject: [subject].

Composition:
- Wide cinematic 16:9 hero.
- Left [percentage] clean copy safe dark graphite negative space.
- Right: [main engineered cell, object, or membrane structure].

Core structure:
- Translucent outer membrane with crisp rim.
- Internal machined organelles and controlled mechanical complexity.
- [If logo based] strict two level relief: lower outer base, raised inner circle.
- [If cavity present] dark architectural center with recessed ground floor
  platform, concentric rings, radial seams, and subtle edge highlights.

Materials:
- brushed titanium
- dark graphite
- glassy black recesses
- polished bevels
- cool white or pale cyan highlights

Lighting and background:
- deep graphite to black cinematic studio
- subtle atmospheric gradient behind subject
- polished black surface with very subtle fading reflection
- sharp high definition, low noise, strong focal clarity

Avoid:
- green line, green orb, central reactor glow
- lens flare, heavy bloom, smoke blanket, particles
- UI panels, labels, readable text
- medical stock illustration, robot brain, cyberpunk city
- floating connectors, semi attached pipes, random extra tiers
```

## Little Organs Calibration

For Little Organs, the style should communicate:

- membrane as just in time context curation
- organelles as specialized internal machinery
- closure as a measurable engineered state
- biology translated into rigorous infrastructure

The best Little Organs image feels like a cell that has been manufactured by a
world class precision engineering lab.
